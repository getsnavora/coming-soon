About Navora

Built for local shopping in Kerala

Navora is Kerala’s own quick-commerce brand. We’re building a platform to connect customers with local stores for grocery delivery and local shopping.

Our aim is to make everyday local shopping faster and easier. Navora’s first planned launch is in Manjeri, Kerala, India.

Our launch status

Navora is coming soon. This website introduces the brand; it does not currently accept grocery orders.

See the Manjeri launch information for what is confirmed and what is still to be announced.

Customers, stores and riders

Local customers, participating stores and delivery riders are central to Navora’s planned local-commerce model. Customer ordering instructions, store participation details and rider information are not yet published on this site.

Navora’s official website

Our official website is navoranow.com. Launch information here will distinguish planned availability from an active service.
