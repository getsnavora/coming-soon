Navora is coming to Manjeri

Manjeri, in Kerala’s Malappuram district, is Navora’s first planned launch location. We’re building a quick-commerce platform for grocery delivery and shopping from local stores.

Is Navora available in Manjeri?

Navora is coming soon. Ordering is not available on this website yet. A launch date and exact delivery coverage have not been announced here.

Please do not assume that every address in Manjeri, or elsewhere in Kerala, will be covered at launch. Check this page for confirmed availability before planning an order.

What will Navora offer?

Navora’s planned focus is groceries and local shopping. The participating stores, product range, delivery charges and delivery estimates are still to be announced.

How will ordering work?

The planned model connects customers with local stores and delivery. Step-by-step ordering instructions, app availability and a way to check delivery availability for your address have not yet been published.

Local stores and delivery riders

Store participation and rider opportunities are part of the planned service. This coming-soon site does not yet offer store or rider registration.
